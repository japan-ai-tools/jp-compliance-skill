# 景品表示法 (Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations) - ad copy

Regulator: Consumer Affairs Agency (消費者庁). Liability sits with the **advertiser** (the business selling the product).

## 1. Stealth marketing (ステマ) - since 2023-10-01
- An ad that consumers cannot recognise as an ad is a violation (景品表示法 第5条第3号 and the designated notice).
  Influencers/affiliates asked by the business are not the regulated party; the business is.
  Source: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/stealth_marketing/
- The regulated party is the advertiser. Content an affiliate writes entirely on their own judgement may fall outside it;
  content the advertiser asked for, paid for or edited is inside. jpcheck therefore says "may violate" and asks you to
  confirm who was involved.
- Triggers in jpcheck: affiliate links (Amazon `tag=`, amzn.to, A8, もしも, 楽天 hb.afl / a.r10.to, ValueCommerce,
  AccessTrade…), or words like 「提供を受け」「いただきました」「案件」「タイアップ」. 「広告ではありません」 next to such a
  link is itself flagged.
- Fix: a clear marker at the top: 【PR】 / 広告 / プロモーション / 「本記事は○○から提供を受けて書いています」.
  A marker only at the very end, in tiny type, or buried among many hashtags may not be "clear".

## 2. #1 / ranking claims
- The CAA published a survey report on #1 (ranking) claims on 2024-09-26 and treats claims without an objective, fair basis
  as misleading (優良誤認). Source: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/survey
- Before keeping a claim, the owner must answer: compared with which products? on what measure? who was surveyed
  (real users, not people shown a list of names)? when? who ran the survey? Put that note right next to the claim.
- Safer rewrite: delete the ranking; state a verifiable fact. 「満足度1位」→「2025年に使った方へのアンケートで、回答者120人中96人が『満足』と回答(自社調べ・2025年4〜9月)」 - only if that survey exists.

## 3. Absolute / effect claims
- 「絶対」「必ず」「100%」「確実に儲かる」 promise results that vary. The CAA can demand reasonable evidence; if it
  is not submitted within 15 days the claim is treated as misleading (景品表示法 第7条第2項, 不実証広告規制).
- Rewrite: describe conditions and what was measured. 「必ず痩せる」→「1日○分の運動と合わせて使った場合の記録です。結果には個人差があります」 (and only if true).
- Health/body effects (治る, 痩せる, シミが消える…) also fall under 薬機法 / 健康増進法 → out of scope, send to a specialist.

## 4. Double pricing (二重価格)
- A comparison price (当社通常価格, 定価, 「50%OFF」) is misleading (有利誤認, 第5条第2号) unless it is real.
  Yardstick for "our regular price" in the CAA price guideline: sold at that price for **more than half of the 8 weeks
  before the sale** (or of the whole sales period, if it has been on sale for less than 8 weeks), **for at least 2 weeks
  in total**, and **no more than 2 weeks have passed since it was last sold at that price**.
  Source: https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/guideline/pdf/100121premiums_35.pdf
- 「メーカー希望小売価格」 must be a price the manufacturer really published; 「市場価格」 needs evidence of the actual market.
- If a product has not sold at a price for long enough, write the actual price flow instead:
  「初月100円、2か月目から月980円(税込)。いつでも解約できます」.

## 5. Scarcity
- 「先着100名」「本日限り」「今なら」 must be true: count real slots, end on the stated date, do not extend silently.

## 6. Disclaimers do not cancel claims
- 「※個人の感想です」「効果には個人差があります」 do not make a strong claim acceptable; the CAA judges the overall
  impression (打消し表示). Fix the claim itself.
